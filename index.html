02 · About

A little about me

I'm a UX/UI design lead based in Hyderabad, with 8.5 years of designing enterprise SaaS, web and mobile products across healthcare, FinTech, renewable energy, education, digital media and e-commerce. I hold an MSc in User Experience Design from Kingston University, London.

I enjoy solving complex problems, such as modernising legacy systems, bringing many stakeholders to one shared direction, and designing for people who work in demanding conditions. My process starts with listening, through stakeholder workshops, interviews and field visits, and ends with clear, buildable design that developers can ship with confidence.

As a lead, I plan the work, run client workshops and reviews, mentor designers and keep quality consistent across modules, so the team moves quickly without losing sight of the user.
